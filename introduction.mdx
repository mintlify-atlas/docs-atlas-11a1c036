LexMetra is an AI-assisted compliance platform purpose-built for Legal Metrology officers who inspect packaged commodities in the field. Rather than manually cross-checking every label against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, inspectors capture or upload a product photograph and LexMetra instantly extracts all mandatory declarations, scores them for compliance, and generates a shareable inspection record — turning a tedious paper-based process into a reliable, auditable digital workflow.

## What is LexMetra?

The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 require every pre-packaged commodity sold in India to carry eight specific declarations on its label. A missing or illegible declaration is a statutory violation, and detecting these violations accurately — across hundreds of SKUs per inspection round — is a significant burden for field officers.

LexMetra addresses this directly. It presents each of the eight mandatory declarations as a structured checklist, populates the fields via OCR analysis of the product image, and immediately flags anything that is absent, unreadable, or requires human review.

The eight declarations checked on every inspection are:

| # | Declaration | Example |
|---|-------------|---------|
| 1 | **Product name** | Tata Salt |
| 2 | **Manufacturer / Packer name & address** | Tata Consumer Products Ltd. |
| 3 | **Net quantity** | 1 kg |
| 4 | **Maximum Retail Price (MRP)** | ₹28.00 |
| 5 | **Packed-on date** | 06/2026 |
| 6 | **Best-before / expiry** | 12 months from packing |
| 7 | **Consumer care number** | 1800-108-4488 |
| 8 | **Country of origin** | India |

Each field receives a confidence score and a status of `VERIFIED`, `MISSING`, or `REVIEW`, making it immediately clear which declarations warrant further investigation.

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI OCR Extraction" icon="scan" href="/features/ocr-extraction">
    Capture or upload a product photograph and the OCR engine automatically extracts all eight mandatory declarations from the label text, annotating each with a confidence percentage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance Scoring" icon="chart-bar" href="/features/compliance-rules">
    Every inspection is assigned a numeric compliance score (0–100) and an overall status of **COMPLIANT**, **VIOLATION**, or **UNCERTAIN**, giving officers an instant at-a-glance verdict.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inspection History" icon="clock-rotate-left" href="/features/history-register">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance Register" icon="book" href="/features/history-register">
    Saved inspections are promoted to the compliance register, creating the persistent, auditable log required for Legal Metrology departmental records and enforcement proceedings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evidence Regions" icon="crosshairs" href="/features/inspection">
    The inspection result highlights bounding-box evidence regions on the product image — showing exactly where on the label each declaration was detected, supporting dispute resolution.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inspection Reports" icon="file-lines" href="/features/inspection">
    Each inspection generates a structured report containing the declaration checklist, evidence annotations, compliance score, and inspector timestamp that can be exported or printed.

## Project Context

LexMetra was built for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**, addressing Problem Statement **PS 26034** issued by the Department of Legal Metrology. The platform was developed by **"The Inspectors"** team with the goal of digitalising field inspection workflows and reducing the manual overhead placed on Legal Metrology officers across India.

<Info>
  The project is actively under development. The OCR engine in the current release uses a client-side mock service that returns realistic demo data, allowing the full inspection workflow to be exercised without a live AI backend. A production OCR integration is planned for the next milestone.
</Info>
