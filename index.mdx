LexMetra is a full-stack compliance platform built for Legal Metrology officers to inspect packaged commodities in the field. Upload or capture a product label image and LexMetra automatically extracts statutory declarations—MRP, net quantity, manufacturer details, country of origin, and more—then scores them against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Inspectors authenticate with their email and password. Sessions are managed server-side using Better Auth and stored in PostgreSQL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan a product">
    Point the device camera at the product label or upload an image from the gallery. LexMetra accepts JPEG and PNG via live capture or file upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review extracted declarations">
    The OCR engine extracts the eight mandatory declarations—product name, manufacturer, net quantity, MRP, packed-on date, best-before, consumer care number, and country of origin—and assigns a confidence score to each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the outcome">
    Inspections are classified as **Compliant**, **Violation**, or **Uncertain**. Save the result to your local inspection history or promote it to the statutory Compliance Register with one tap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key capabilities

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="AI OCR Extraction" icon="scan">
    Automatically detects and extracts statutory label fields from product images with per-field confidence scores.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance Scoring" icon="shield-check">
    Rules engine maps extracted declarations to Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules and flags missing or incomplete fields.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evidence Regions" icon="crosshairs">
    Bounding-box overlays on the captured image link each extracted declaration back to its physical location on the label.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inspection History" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Every scan is logged with a unique inspection ID, timestamp, and full declaration breakdown for audit purposes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance Register" icon="clipboard-check">
    Verified products can be promoted to a searchable statutory register, distinct from the general inspection history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Downloadable Reports" icon="file-arrow-down">
    Generate a formatted inspection report for any record, ready for field evidence or supervisory review.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  LexMetra was built for **SIH 2026 Problem Statement PS 26034** by The Inspectors team. The current release uses a demo OCR service; the production OCR pipeline is under active development.
</Note>
